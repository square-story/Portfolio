## Overview

Blipko is an innovative solution designed to simplify personal finance management. By leveraging the ubiquity of WhatsApp, it allows users to track their expenses and income as naturally as chatting with a friend.

## Key Features

- **Natural Language Input**: Type "Spent 50 on lunch" and it's recorded.
- **Voice Commands**: meaningful expense tracking on the go.
- **Image Recognition**: Snap a photo of a receipt to log it automatically.
- **Monthly Reports**: Get automated summaries of your financial health.

## Technologies

Built with a robust stack ensuring reliability and scalability:
- **Node.js** for the backend logic.
- **WhatsApp Cloud API** for the messaging interface.
- **OpenAI** for parsing natural language and receipt images.
- **PostgreSQL** for secure and structured data storage.
